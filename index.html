The heart behind Ruby7Art

A creative soul.
A God-given gift.

I was about four when I watched my cousin draw and decided to try it myself. My first subject was a little collectible token from a bag of chips. Seeing my drawing come to life made me want to try again, and again. That small moment became a lifelong love for creating.

As a self-taught artist, I have grown through practice, new techniques, and the inspiration of other artists. My faith is woven into that journey. Creating reminds me of God, our Creator, and the blessing of bringing an idea to life with my own hands.

A little more of my story

The story of Bezalel, an artisan God filled with His Spirit and equipped to create, especially inspires me. I see this talent as a gift to share. Each Ruby7Art creation carries a piece of my creative soul, but also something of your story. Helping you express love, celebrate someone, or hold onto a memory is what makes this work so meaningful to me.
